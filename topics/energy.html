Conservation of energy

Energy changes form but, in an isolated system, its total stays the same.

Energy appears as motion (kinetic energy, ½mv²), position in a field (near Earth's surface a mass m raised a height h has gravitational potential energy mgh), heat, light, chemical bonds and more. A falling ball turns potential energy into kinetic energy; friction turns some of it into heat. The total, counting the heat, does not change.

The SI unit is the joule: the energy transferred when a force of one newton acts through one metre. The calorie used in chemistry and nutrition is a different unit, defined by the exact conversion below.
