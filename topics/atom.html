Chemistry

The atom

The smallest unit of an element: a dense nucleus of protons and neutrons surrounded by electrons.

The number of protons, the atomic number, decides which element an atom is. Atoms of one element with different numbers of neutrons are isotopes. Electrons carry negative charge and occupy regions around the nucleus; a neutral atom has as many electrons as protons. Almost all of an atom's mass is in its nucleus.

Chemistry is mostly the behaviour of the outermost electrons: how atoms share or transfer them to form bonds.
