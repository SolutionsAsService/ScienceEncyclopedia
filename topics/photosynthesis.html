Photosynthesis

How plants, algae and some bacteria turn light, water and carbon dioxide into sugar and oxygen.

In plants it takes place in chloroplasts. The light reactions use light to split water, releasing oxygen and storing energy in ATP and NADPH. The Calvin cycle uses that energy to fix carbon dioxide into sugars. The overall change can be summarised as 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂.

Nearly all the energy that living things use at the surface of Earth comes from sunlight captured this way.
