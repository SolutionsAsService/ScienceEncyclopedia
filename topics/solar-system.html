Earth and space

The Solar System

The Sun and everything held by its gravity, including eight planets.

In order from the Sun the planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune. The first four are rocky; Jupiter and Saturn are mostly hydrogen and helium; Uranus and Neptune are ice giants. The system also holds dwarf planets, moons, asteroids and comets.

Distances inside the Solar System are measured in astronomical units, defined from the average distance between Earth and the Sun.
