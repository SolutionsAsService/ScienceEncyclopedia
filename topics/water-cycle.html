Earth and space

The water cycle

The continuous movement of water between oceans, air, land and living things.

The Sun's heat evaporates water from oceans, lakes and soil, and plants release more through transpiration. The vapour rises, cools and condenses into clouds, and falls again as rain or snow. On land the water runs off into rivers, soaks into the ground, or is stored as ice, and most of it eventually returns to the sea.

Most of Earth's water is in the oceans; a small share is fresh water, and much of that is held in ice sheets and glaciers.
