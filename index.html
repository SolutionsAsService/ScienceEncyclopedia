How sure is that number?

A constant that is defined (299 792 458 m/s) and one that has to be measured (6.674 × 10⁻¹¹) are different kinds of number, and are often printed as if they were not. Here the figures listed under each topic, and in the constants table, carry a mark. Numbers in the running text and the dates do not.

  • Speed of light in a vacuum299 792 458 m/sExact
  • Gravitational constant, G6.674 30(15) × 10⁻¹¹ m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻²Measured
  • GPS clock drift from relativityabout 38 µs gained per dayRounded
