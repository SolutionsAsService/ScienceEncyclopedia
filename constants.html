Constants, and how well each is known

Seven defining constants of the SI, five exact conventions, and five constants known by measurement (one shown with its uncertainty, four rounded). Exact values are fixed by definition of the SI (2019) or by convention; the current precise measured values are published by CODATA.

The three marks

  • ExactExact by definition or by agreed convention. The number is part of how the unit or constant is defined, so it has no uncertainty.
  • MeasuredFound by experiment. The figure in brackets is the uncertainty in the last digits, and later measurements may refine it.
  • RoundedShown to fewer digits than it is known, or a typical value that varies from case to case. Rounded does not mean poorly known: the electron's mass is known far better than the gravitational constant. Check the source for more digits.
