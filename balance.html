How it works

Each element gives one linear equation: atoms on the left minus atoms on the right equals zero. The coefficients are the solution of that system, found with exact fractions (no rounding), scaled to the smallest whole numbers.

If the system has no solution, or more than one independent solution, it says so instead of guessing.

What it accepts

  • Formulas such as Ca(OH)2, Fe2(SO4)3, K4[Fe(CN)6] and hydrates like CuSO4·5H2O.
  • Arrows ->, = or →.
  • Not yet: ions and charges, state symbols such as (aq), and isotopes.
  • Capital letters matter: Co is cobalt, CO is carbon monoxide.
